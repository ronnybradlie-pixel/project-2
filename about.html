Who We Are

FlickerFeed is a modern movie discovery platform built to help users explore the latest movies, trending ones, and personalized recommendations.We have to bring entertainment closer to you.

What We Offer

  • Trending movies updated daily
  • Personalized favorites list (saved in local storage)
  • Movie categories like Action, Drama & Comedy
  • Smart search powered by TMDB API
  • Light & Dark mode theme
  • User profiles with login & signup

To create an easy, fun, and powerful platform for discovering movies, connecting fans, and giving users control over their entertainment preferences.
